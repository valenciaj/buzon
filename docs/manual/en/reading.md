# Reading mail

## The conversation list

Buzón groups messages on the same topic into a **conversation**. The list shows who wrote, the subject, the start of the text and the date. Unread conversations are in bold.

- Tap a conversation to open it. On a computer it opens next to the list; on a phone, full screen.
- To pick several, tick each one's checkbox (or press <kbd>x</kbd>) and use the buttons at the top.
- More conversations load when you reach the end of the list.

At the top of the sidebar you choose the account. **All** shows the mail from all your accounts together; each conversation has a dot with its account's color.

## Reading a conversation

Messages are shown oldest to newest, with the last one open. Tap a message's header to open or close it.

**Safe reading.** Buzón cleans the HTML of every email and shows it isolated from the rest of the page, so an email can't run code or trick you with forms.

**Remote images.** So nobody knows when you open their email, Buzón doesn't load images from the internet until you click **Show images**. It also removes **tracking pixels** (invisible images that tell the sender you've read the email) and tells you who they came from.

**Attachments.** They appear at the end of each message. Click one to download it; images and PDFs can be viewed in the browser.

## Live updates

No need to reload. Buzón listens to your mail server and new mail appears as soon as it arrives. If an account has a problem (for example, the password changed), you'll see it in the sidebar and in **Settings › Email accounts**.
