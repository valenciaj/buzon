# Today, scheduled and reminders

## Today

**Today** (<kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>t</kbd>) sums up your day on one screen:

- How many unread emails you have in Main (or in the inbox, in Classic mode), including the ones bubbling up.
- How many senders are waiting in the **Screener** and how many conversations you have in **Reply Later**.
- Conversations with **no reply** whose deadline has passed.
- **Scheduled** emails leaving in the next 24 hours.

Tap any tile to go to that box.

![The Today page](/img/en/hoy.png)

## Scheduled

Emails you've scheduled wait in **Scheduled**. You can **change** them (they go back to the compose window) or **cancel send** (they move to Drafts). If a send fails, it goes back to Drafts with the reason.

## No reply

When you send an email with **Remind me if there's no reply**, Buzón watches the conversation. If the deadline passes with no reply, it shows up in **No reply**, with a count in the sidebar. If you're no longer waiting, click **Stop waiting**.

## Command palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) and type what you want to do: "trash", "compose", "dark theme", "settings"… You don't need accents or the whole word. If nothing matches, the last option searches your email for that text.

![Command palette](/img/en/paleta.png)

## Notifications

In **Settings › Appearance › Sending and notifications**, turn on **Notifications** so the browser tells you about new email when you're not looking at Buzón. In Focus mode it only notifies you about mail arriving in Main, so newsletters don't interrupt you.
