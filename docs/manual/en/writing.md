# Writing

## Composing an email

Click the round pencil button **Compose**, next to the logo or at the bottom right (or <kbd>c</kbd>). A window opens with:

- **From:** the address you send from, if you have several accounts or identities.
- **To, Cc and Bcc:** type a name or an address; Buzón suggests your contacts. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or a comma to add it. Cc and Bcc fold away with their ×.
- **Subject** and **message**, with bold, italics, lists, quotes and links.

![Composing an email](/img/en/redactar.png)

While you write, Buzón saves the **draft** automatically. If you close the window, you'll find it in **Drafts**. A copy is also saved to the Drafts folder on your mail server (a few seconds after you stop typing), so you can carry on from your phone or another app.

## Attachments

Click the paperclip or drag files onto the window. Each file can be up to 25 MB.

## Reply and forward

Inside a conversation you have **Reply** (<kbd>r</kbd>), **Reply all** (<kbd>a</kbd>) and **Forward** (<kbd>f</kbd>). Buzón quotes the original message and keeps the conversation together.

## Undo send

After clicking **Send** you have a few seconds to change your mind: a notice with **Undo** appears. Click it and the email goes back to the compose window. Change the time in **Settings › Appearance › Sending and notifications** (0 to 30 seconds).

## Schedule send

Click the clock next to **Send** and choose when it should go out: this evening, tomorrow, this weekend, next week, or any date and time you like. The email waits in **Scheduled** and you can change or cancel it until then. It goes out even if your browser is closed.

## Remind me if there's no reply

Click the bell next to **Send** and choose how many days (1, 3, 7 or 14). If nobody replies in that time, the conversation shows up in **No reply**. If someone replies sooner, the reminder disappears on its own.

## Read receipt

Click the double tick next to **Send** to ask the recipient to confirm they've read the email; the button stays blue. Their email program decides whether to ask them and whether to send it: many don't. If it arrives, you'll see it as a regular email.

## Signatures and identities

In **Settings › Identities & signatures** you can:

- Change the name people see when you send.
- Add aliases: other addresses your server lets you use.
- Write a signature for each address. It's added at the end automatically, after "-- ".
