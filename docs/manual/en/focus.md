# Focus mode

Focus mode puts you back in control of your inbox. It's inspired by Hey: email doesn't just walk in; first you decide who gets to write to you.

## Screener: who can email you

The first time someone emails you, their message doesn't go to your inbox: it waits in the **Screener**. For each sender you decide:

- **Yes**, and where their email goes: **Main**, **News** or **Receipts**.
- **No**: you won't see their email in your boxes again. It's kept in **Screened out** in case you change your mind.

Your contacts, and people you write to, are approved automatically. Mail you already had before connecting the account doesn't go through the Screener either.

![Senders in the Screener](/img/en/por-aprobar.png)

## Main, News and Receipts

- **Main:** people and what really matters.
- **News:** newsletters, promotions and notifications. Skim them when it suits you.
- **Receipts:** invoices, orders, shipping and tickets.

You can change where a sender's email goes at any time from the header of their conversation.

## The piles

For things you can't deal with right now:

- **Reply Later** (<kbd>l</kbd>): conversations you need to answer. They leave the pile once you reply. **Focus & reply** opens them one after another so you can get through them in one go.
- **Set Aside** (<kbd>p</kbd>): things you want to keep handy (a booking, an order number…).
- **Snoozed** (<kbd>b</kbd>, until tomorrow): they disappear and **bubble up** to the top of Main on the day and time you choose.

## Private notes

On any conversation you can write a **note** only you can see: "call on Tuesday", "quote approved"…

## Tracking pixels

Buzón removes the invisible images some senders use to know whether you open their email, and tells you who they came from.
